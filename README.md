# One Line of Earned Pride

A single line of text, 1 to 240 characters: a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene — a small person doing something hard, a long effort finally landing. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557/requirements

## Entry

After forty years of night shifts, my father finally watched his daughter graduate — and for once, he let himself be the one clapped for.
